# Analysis decisions

## 2026-10-26 — Patient-level model evaluation and HIV markers

Add score-distribution and ranked-patient panels after each active model-evaluation group in `gene_interactions.ipynb`, after both saved-prediction comparisons in `gene_cutoff_interpretation.ipynb`, and after the prediction check for the SHAP model in `gene_interactions_interpretation.ipynb`. Each cell shows its models together in one figure. The adapter in `Scripts_For_Manuscript/evaluation_panels.py` connects the predictions to the recorded metadata; `model_evaluation_plots.py` renders the same design used by the related repositories.

The visual reference is [Gardella et al., PNAS 2025, Figure 3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12377778/). Its per-patient successful-test fraction comes from repeated evaluations. This repository exports one final prediction per model, split and patient. Therefore these new panels show scores and incorrect decisions; they do not invent repeated-evaluation success rates or treat different classifiers as repeats. Training predictions are marked apparent fit.

HIV status is joined by exact `sample_id` within the original split from `Files_For_Manuscript/metadata.{train,test,val}.tsv`. Positive IDs receive `[HIV+]`; unknown status would receive `[HIV?]`, never a negative label. Recorded HIV-positive counts are 17/130 training, 10/61 test and 10/60 external-validation participants. HIV subgroup metrics are descriptive; a difference does not demonstrate that HIV caused model error. Readable patient PDF pages, an offline searchable table, and exact prediction and subgroup-metric TSVs accompany every combined plot. These generated files remain local in the Git-ignored `Figure_Output/Model_Evaluation/` directory and are regenerated by the notebook plotting cells; patient-ID/HIV detail files are not committed or published. The notebooks retain their embedded summary figures.

Preserve score units. Logistic and Gaussian-naive-Bayes log odds, ridge decisions, and SVM margins remain decision scores; they are not relabeled probabilities. The paper GLM uses its recorded training-derived threshold, strictly `score > 0.497594075975128`. AUC-only classifier comparisons receive explicitly documented default-boundary decisions (strictly >0 for margins and >0.5 for probabilities). No threshold is learned from test or validation outcomes. The GLM newly fit on the XGBoost gene panel uses the default 0.5 threshold; it does not inherit a threshold trained on another gene panel. The transfer-model threshold in another repository is likewise not imported here.

The original R Figure_1/2/3 notebooks remain unchanged. Figure_2 is a hash-checked historical reference input; changing its presentation would break that provenance contract. Its paper-GLM predictions are displayed in the active Python notebook. Historical Figure_1 models represented only by aggregate AUCs cannot supply patient-level plots without missing predictions; none are fabricated.

Validation is limited to the new plotting cells and saved-score adapters, not a rerun of model fitting. The registered `cfrna-tb` kernel points to a currently absent `.venv`; use the available Python kernel for these figure-only checks and preserve the notebooks' original kernel specification. Existing numerical results and frozen references are protected by before/after SHA-256 checks. Completion counts and checks are recorded in `Figure_Output/Model_Evaluation/verification.json`.
